# Metric Prefixes

Metric prefixes are used with units to represent decimal multiples and submultiples of a unit. They make it easier to express very large or very small measurements.

## Common Examples

Metric prefixes can be combined with many SI units.

Examples:

* 1 kilometer (km) = 1,000 meters (m)
* 1 megawatt (MW) = 1,000,000 watts (W)
* 1 millimeter (mm) = 0.001 meter (m)
* 1 microsecond (µs) = 0.000001 second (s)
* 1 nanometer (nm) = 0.000000001 meter (m)

## Large Prefixes

Large prefixes are useful when working with very large quantities.

For example:

**1 gigabyte = 10⁹ bytes**

**1 terawatt = 10¹² watts**

**1 petabyte = 10¹⁵ bytes**

## Small Prefixes

Small prefixes are useful when measuring very small quantities.

For example:

**1 millimeter = 10⁻³ meter**

**1 micrometer = 10⁻⁶ meter**

**1 nanometer = 10⁻⁹ meter**

## Using Metric Prefixes

To convert a prefixed unit into the corresponding base unit, multiply by the prefix factor.

For example:

**5 km × 10³ = 5,000 m**

Similarly:

**5 mm × 10⁻³ = 0.005 m**

Metric prefixes provide a convenient way to express measurements while maintaining a consistent decimal relationship between units.
