Conversion factors are numerical relationships used to convert a measurement from one unit to another within the same physical quantity.

## Temperature

Temperature conversions require formulas rather than a single multiplication factor.

### Celsius and Fahrenheit

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

### Celsius and Kelvin

K = °C + 273.15

°C = K − 273.15

### Fahrenheit and Kelvin

K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15

## Using Conversion Factors

For linear conversions, a value can generally be converted by multiplying it by the appropriate conversion factor.

For example:

**5 kilometers × 1,000 = 5,000 meters**

Temperature conversions are different because Celsius and Fahrenheit use offsets as well as scaling factors.
